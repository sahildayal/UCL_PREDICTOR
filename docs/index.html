What went wrong

Every bug that touched a forecast or a bet, and what changed because of it.

All 5 and the fixes
  • 4 Nov

    A model bet on both teams

    Wednesday games appear in both Tuesday's and Wednesday's forecasts. Elo v2 changed its mind overnight on Lens v Como and backed both sides.

  • 20 Oct

    Matchday 3 had no odds

    The odds feed shared a free quota with another project, and it ran dry. Matchday 3 was still forecast and scored, but nobody placed a bet.

  • 13 Oct

    Nine results the code couldn't read

    Wikipedia wrote matchday 2's scores with a maths minus sign (0−2) instead of a hyphen. Nine finished games looked unplayed, and 47 bets sat open for six days.

Fake money only. No real bets are placed, and the models never see betting odds.
