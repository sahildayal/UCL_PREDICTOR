Flat bet

$100 on the model's favourite in every game that has a market price. Lots of bets, so luck evens out faster. This is the fair test.

Edge only

Bets only when the model rates a result at least a few points more likely than the market does, and stakes more the bigger the gap. Closer to how a real bettor would play.
